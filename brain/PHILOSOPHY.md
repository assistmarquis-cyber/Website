# Marquis's philosophy (working draft)

Built only from what Marquis has written in this project. Nothing is invented. Items marked **[Marquis to fill]** are the parts only he can say.

## The core

- **Belief in the power of an individual.** Every talk and offer hangs on it.
- **Know your voice. Trust your instinct. Act fast.**
- **Specific intelligence:** "the unique gift and understanding of an individual." AI brings general intelligence. You bring specific intelligence.
- **Mindset, Execution, Identity, Decision making, and a personal operating system at the core.** AI frameworks come after that, not first.
- People should leave with **clarity**.

## The mechanics he teaches

1. **Survival voice vs instinct voice.** Pressure shrinks your view so you see only what you want most, and sometimes that costs you. Naming the voice is the skill.
2. **The G.O.D. Framework:** the Generator creates, the Operator executes, the Destroyer eliminates. Mind mastery at the center. His own confession: he skipped the Destroyer most.
3. **The coaching sequence:** listen to what is hardest right now, name the impulses and patterns, prototype a first solution fast, act with an accountability partner.
4. **Be true to your systems.** Everyone is calm before the pressure. The system is what the calm version leaves for the stressed one. (From the $350K deal that fell apart with no contingency plan.)
5. **Who do you want, and have to become, to accomplish your goal?** Moving with clarity.
6. **Ship it. Amplify them.** AI that makes people stronger, not smaller.

ADHD diagnosed at 8, a paid speaker at 38. The $350K airline deal that taught him to be true to his systems. Philly Tech Week Frequency with Tune.FM. Six months in a 9,000 sq ft creative studio in Dallas. Hive, Signature Society, SolidArt.
