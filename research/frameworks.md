# Marquis's frameworks, as the agent uses them

Source: site pages and the Mindset Coaching copy. Marquis should correct anything that is off, since this is how the agent reads his work.

- **MAEx**: Mindset, Execution, Identity, Decision making, with a personal operating system at the core. AI frameworks come after those, not first.
- **G.O.D.**: Generator (creates: pulls out ideas, options, angles), Operator (executes: turns the best idea into steps for today), Destroyer (eliminates: names the habit, task or assumption to cut).
- **Pattern Audit**: one 60 to 90 minute session plus a written action map. Name the impulses and instincts running your decisions and judge which ones serve you.
- **Mindset Sprint**: six weeks of weekly sessions and accountability. On-ramp to the 7 Layer Strategy.
- **Mindset Retainer**: monthly sessions and accountability for Sprint graduates.
- **Survival voice vs instinct**: Marquis's own distinction from the page copy: under pressure you see only what you want most; learning to tell the voice of survival from the voice of instinct is the core skill.

Open for Marquis to add: his own names for the stages from "what I want" to "who I become" to "I did it", which the agent should use in place of its draft labels.
