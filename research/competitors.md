## 1. Platform guidance on hooks and CTAs (primary, from the platforms)
| Platform | What they say | Source |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok (ads guidance) | Grab attention in the first 6 seconds with suspense, surprise or emotion; state the core message within 3 seconds; use sound; keep the look casual and native rather than polished; end with a clear call to action. ## 3. Hormozi's public teaching (secondary sources; book published 2021 per SuperSummary)
- **Value equation.** Value goes up with a bigger dream outcome and higher perceived likelihood of achieving it; value goes down with longer time delay and more effort and sacrifice. Source: https://www.supersummary.com/100m-offers/section-3-summary/
- **Guarantees.** Three types described: unconditional (refund window), conditional (tied to the buyer completing steps), outcome-based (payment tied to a result). Source: https://www.shortform.com/blog/alex-hormozi-guarantees/
- **Offer stacking and bonuses.** Break the offer into parts and present extras as bonuses so the core price is not discounted; each bonus should answer one reason the buyer might fail. Pricing on value delivered, not cost; a specific niche supports a higher price. Source: https://www.maxmednik.com/blog/notes-on-100m-offers-by-alex-hormozi
- **Scarcity and urgency.** The same notes list capped seats, cohort start dates and deadlines. That source does not discuss honesty; real capacity and real dates are the ethical version (see section 5).

## 4. Gary Vaynerchuk's public teaching
- **Document, don't create; pillar and micro-content.** Third-party explainer: share what you are already doing, build recurring large "pillar" pieces (a vlog, a Q&A show), cut them into short pieces for each channel; use a set schedule instead of deciding daily. Source: https://getgist.com/gary-vee-content-model/ (read 2026-10-29). That article does not use the word "pyramid"; the structure matches.
- **Jab, jab, jab, right hook** (2013 book, from my own knowledge, **not re-verified this run**): give value several times before making an ask. My fetch of a summary page failed, so confirm against the book before using the phrase publicly.

## 5. What Alan will not copy, and why
| Tactic | Why not | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Fake or undisclosed testimonials and reviews, hiding bad reviews | FTC final rule on reviews and testimonials, announced Aug 14 2024, effective Oct 21 2024, bans fake or false reviews, undisclosed insider reviews, and misleading suppression of reviews. | https://www.ftc.gov/node/86152 and https://www.insideprivacy.com/advertising-marketing/ftc-issues-final-rule-on-reviews-and-testimonials/ |
| Income or "you will get rich" guarantees | FTC notices say businesses cannot make false or misleading earnings claims, falsely say no experience is needed, or call a money-making offer risk-free; penalties up to $43,792 per violation. Also contrary to the site's "no guarantee of results" line. | https://consumer.ftc.gov/node/298538 |
| Fake scarcity or countdowns that reset | The same notices flag falsely pressuring buyers to act immediately. Use only real capacity (Marquis's Sprint capacity is 3 a week) and real dates. | https://consumer.ftc.gov/node/298538 |
| Therapy or medical promises | Marquis's coaching is not therapy; keep the existing disclaimer. | Mindset Coaching page copy |
| Quoting results of other people as if they were Marquis's | Use only real client results with permission, labelled as individual and not typical. | FTC links above |

I am not a lawyer; check the FTC points with counsel before launch.

## 6. Ideas Alan can use that fit Marquis (draft, his call)
1. One free, low-friction step: the Pattern Audit prompt or a short loop quiz, with email capture, then straight to the offer.
2. Make the value equation the offer page spine: name the outcome, show the first early win, cut effort.
3. Show proof as named clients and permitted quotes, and label anything unverified as claimed.
4. Keep prices visible (competitors hide them; this is a choice, not a given).
5. Content: document real work (Marquis's 7 first-person titles) as pillar content, cut short pieces from it.
