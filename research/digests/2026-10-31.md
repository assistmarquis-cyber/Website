# Research digest, 2026-10-31

Added 2 verified sources (library now 15) and 2 loops (12 total). A third queue item, Oettingen's identity-based motivation paper by Oyserman (2009), is confirmed to exist but I did not read its abstract, so it stays in the queue.

- **Gross & John 2003**: habitual reframing went with more positive emotion, better relationships and higher well-being; habitual holding-in went with the reverse. New loop: the swallow-it loop. Caveat: questionnaire data, shows association.
- **A-Tjak et al. 2015** (meta-analysis, 39 trials): acceptance and commitment therapy beat waitlist, usual care and placebo, and matched CBT. New loop: the thought-is-a-fact loop. Caveat: clinical trials for diagnosed problems; coaching must refer out and must not claim the same effect.

Portal suggestion: when a client types a thought like "I can't", offer a one-tap rewrite to "I'm having the thought that...".
