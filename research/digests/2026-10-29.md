# Research digest, 2026-10-29 (first run)

Seeded 10 verified sources and 9 negative loops, each loop mapped to a pillar, tool and G.O.D. role.

The 3 things the evidence says most clearly for Alan:
1. The say-do gap closes by rewriting the story, not the behavior, unless something interrupts it (Festinger & Carlsmith 1959). Alan's job is to make the gap visible without shaming.
2. "If X then I do Y" plans have the strongest evidence for turning a wanted goal into action (Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006, meta-analysis).
3. Goals that are genuinely the person's own get more effort and pay back more well-being (Sheldon & Elliot 1999). That supports Alan asking whose voice is behind a goal.

Guardrails added: willpower-as-tank is contested (Hagger 2016 replication near zero); growth mindset effects are real but small and context-dependent (Yeager 2019); "21 days" is a myth (Lally 2010 median 66 days).
