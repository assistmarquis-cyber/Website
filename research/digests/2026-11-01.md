- **Macnamara, Hambrick & Oswald 2014** (meta-analysis, 88 studies): deliberate practice explained about 12% of performance differences overall, much less in professions. New loop: the hours myth. Caveat: I verified it through a Princeton news summary, so the figures come from that summary, and the finding is contested.
- **Holt-Lunstad, Smith & Layton 2010** (meta-analysis, 148 studies): stronger social relationships went with about 50% higher odds of survival. New loop: going it alone. Caveat: it is about survival, not goal follow-through, so Alan must not claim accountability partners raise success rates from this paper.
