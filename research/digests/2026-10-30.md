# Research digest, 2026-10-30

Added 3 verified sources (library now 13) and 1 new loop (10 total).

- **Oettingen & Mayer 2002**: positive expectations predicted effort and results; pleasant fantasies about the outcome predicted less effort. Supports the "wish without the obstacle" loop and Alan asking about the obstacle, not just the dream. Caveat: small, correlational follow-ups.
- **Ryan & Deci 2000** (theory review): autonomy, competence and relatedness support self-motivation. Backs the "whose goal is this" move, and suggests Alan should check relatedness (who is with you) as well as the goal.
- **Breines & Chen 2012**: self-compassion after a failure raised motivation to improve across four experiments. New loop added: the harsh inner critic. Caveat: lab studies, short-term measures.

Portal suggestion: add a quiet "who is in this with you?" prompt after the goal step.
