## 3. Guarantees

Three types exist: unconditional refund window, conditional (tied to the buyer doing the work), outcome-based. The site currently says "no guarantee of results", and the FTC treats income or result guarantees seriously. Options for Marquis and counsel: a **show-up guarantee** (if the Pattern Audit does not give you a clear written next step, you get a refund) or none. Alan promises nothing about results.

## 7. What the platforms say about hooks (primary sources only)

TikTok's ad guidance: grab attention in about 6 seconds, state the core message within 3, keep it native and casual, end with one clear call to action. YouTube measures the intro as the share still watching at 30 seconds and says the opening must match the title and thumbnail. Alan's reel script follows that (hook in 2 seconds, loop, moment, move, ask, link). Observed creator hooks: **not gathered**; someone with a browser needs to log the first 10 seconds of 10 to 20 top mindset videos.

## 8. What Alan will not copy

Fake or undisclosed testimonials, hidden bad reviews, income or "get rich" claims, fake scarcity and resetting countdowns, risk-free claims, therapy or medical promises. The FTC points come from the research thread's sources; check them with counsel.
